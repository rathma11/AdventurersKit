Adventurer's Kit does not bundle code from other addons. It reads Questie's
quest database while the game runs (Questie must be installed separately) and
carries small copies of the following data so it does not depend on more
addons:

Questie (GPL-3.0) - https://github.com/Questie/Questie
  The Questie team: Aero, Logon, Muehe, TheCrux (BreakBB), Drejjmit, Dyaxler,
  Cheeq, TechnoHunter, Yttrium and all contributors.
  Used: the quest, NPC, object and item database at runtime; a few dungeon
  entrance coordinates (Zones\dungeons.lua, Forever) copied into
  Planner\DungeonEntrances.lua.

DungeonJournal (ForeverDungeonJournal) by Exehn
  Used: dungeon bosses with NPC IDs (Planner\DungeonBosses.lua), WoW: Forever's
  own dungeons with their quests and level ranges (Planner\DungeonQuests.lua)
  and dungeon entrance coordinates (Planner\DungeonEntrances.lua), from its
  Data\Dungeons.lua and Data\DungeonEntrances.lua. When DungeonJournal is
  installed, Adventurer's Kit can open it (Shift+click a dungeon).
